# Landing Page Brand Script

This brand script frames the landing page around a clear founder journey: the founder is the hero, fundraising drag is the problem, and RaiseSignal is the agent that runs the capital-raise workflow so founders can stay focused on building.

## One-Liner

RaiseSignal is the fundraising agent founders can hand their raise to: it builds the investor strategy, prepares the materials, personalizes outreach, and keeps the process moving.

## The Character

Startup founders who are building something ambitious and need to raise capital.

They want:

- A capital raise that moves without becoming their full-time job
- A clear, ranked list of investors who actually fit their company
- A stronger pitch and investor narrative
- Personalized outreach and follow-up that does not sound generic
- Research that prepares them for every investor conversation
- A system that tracks next steps and keeps momentum across the raise

## The Problem

Raising capital is still too manual, fragmented, and relationship-driven.

External problems:

- Founders do not know which investors are the best fit.
- Investor research is scattered across databases, websites, social posts, podcasts, and warm-intro networks.
- Pitch feedback is hard to get before the stakes are real.
- Outreach often sounds generic.
- VC questions can expose weak spots in the story.
- Follow-ups, CRM updates, meeting prep, and next steps are easy to drop while running the company.

Internal problems:

- Founders feel unsure whether they are targeting the right investors.
- They worry their deck is not telling the strongest version of the story.
- They feel underprepared for investor objections.
- They are tired of wasting time on investors who were never a fit.
- They feel like fundraising creates a second operating system they have to manage by hand.

Philosophical problem:

Great founders should not lose momentum because raising capital requires too much manual work, fragmented knowledge, and hidden process.

## The Guide

RaiseSignal gives founders a fundraising agent that can turn startup context into an active capital-raise process.

Empathy:

We know fundraising can feel like a second company you have to operate while still running the first one.

Authority:

RaiseSignal combines curated investor context, AI research, pitch analysis, outreach generation, meeting prep, and workflow automation to help founders move from "we need to raise" to a focused investor process.

## The Plan

1. Tell the agent what you are raising.
   Add your company description, stage, sector, traction, geography, and fundraising goals.

2. Let RaiseSignal build the raise.
   The agent ranks investor targets, analyzes your deck, prepares outreach, researches each investor, and organizes the next steps.

3. Run the process with momentum.
   Review the agent's recommendations, approve outreach, enter meetings prepared, and keep every follow-up moving.

## Calls To Action

Primary CTA:

- Start my raise

Secondary CTAs:

- Build my investor list
- Analyze my deck
- Draft investor outreach
- Prepare me for VC questions

## Stakes

What founders avoid:

- Wasting weeks researching the wrong investors
- Sending generic cold emails that get ignored
- Walking into meetings without investor-specific context
- Discovering pitch weaknesses only after investor calls
- Losing confidence when hard questions come up
- Letting follow-ups and next steps slip because the process lives across too many tools

What founders gain:

- A focused investor target list
- A clearer fundraising narrative
- More relevant outreach
- Better meeting prep
- A more automated fundraising workflow
- Confidence in the next investor conversation and the next step after it

## Section Copy

### Stop Fundraising In The Dark

Most founders spend too much time guessing which investors to contact, what to say, what to fix in the deck, and what to do next. RaiseSignal turns scattered fundraising work into an automated agent-led process.

### Find Investors Who Fit

The agent matches your startup against investor stage, sector, check size, geography, thesis, and portfolio context so you can focus on the people most likely to care.

### Make Your Pitch Investor-Ready

Upload your deck and the agent reviews the story investors evaluate: problem, market, traction, team, ask, and narrative strength.

### Send Outreach That Feels Personal

Generate cold emails, warm intro requests, and follow-ups that use investor-specific context instead of generic templates.

### Practice The Questions That Matter

Train against hard VC questions and get feedback on the substance, clarity, and delivery of your answers.

### Walk Into Meetings Prepared

Before each call, the agent prepares a concise "why them, why now" brief, portfolio context, conversation hooks, and likely objections.

### Keep The Raise Moving

Track investor status, recommended next steps, follow-ups, and meeting prep in one workflow so the process does not stall between conversations.

## Voice And Tone

- Clear, direct, and founder-friendly
- Confident without hype
- Practical over inspirational
- Specific about outcomes
- Respectful of how high-stakes fundraising feels
- More autonomous than advisory

## Copy Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed funding or investor introductions.
- Do not imply the tool replaces founder judgment or sends high-stakes messages without founder approval.
- Avoid generic AI language like "supercharge" or "revolutionize."
- Keep copy focused on automation, momentum, preparation, and fit.
- Position the founder as the hero and RaiseSignal as the agent operating the raise.
