# Landing Page Brand Script

This brand script frames the landing page around a clear founder journey: the founder is the hero, fundraising confusion is the problem, and RaiseSignal is the guide that helps them raise with focus and confidence.

## One-Liner

RaiseSignal helps startup founders find the right investors, sharpen their pitch, and walk into fundraising conversations prepared.

## The Character

Startup founders who are building something ambitious and need to raise capital.

They want:

- A clear list of investors who actually fit their company
- A stronger pitch deck
- Better investor outreach
- Confidence answering hard VC questions
- Research that helps them show up prepared for every meeting

## The Problem

Fundraising is noisy, time-consuming, and full of hidden expectations.

External problems:

- Founders do not know which investors are the best fit.
- Investor research is scattered across databases, websites, social posts, podcasts, and warm-intro networks.
- Pitch feedback is hard to get before the stakes are real.
- Outreach often sounds generic.
- VC questions can expose weak spots in the story.

Internal problems:

- Founders feel unsure whether they are targeting the right investors.
- They worry their deck is not telling the strongest version of the story.
- They feel underprepared for investor objections.
- They are tired of wasting time on investors who were never a fit.

Philosophical problem:

Great founders should not lose momentum because fundraising knowledge is fragmented, relationship-driven, and hard to access.

## The Guide

RaiseSignal gives founders a practical fundraising copilot built around investor fit, sharper storytelling, and better meeting prep.

Empathy:

We know fundraising can feel like a second company you have to build while still running the first one.

Authority:

RaiseSignal combines curated investor context, AI-assisted research, pitch analysis, and outreach support to help founders focus on the investors and messages that matter.

## The Plan

1. Share your startup context.
   Add your company description, stage, sector, traction, geography, and fundraising goals.

2. Build your investor strategy.
   Get matched with relevant investors, analyze your deck, generate tailored outreach, and research target investors before meetings.

3. Raise with confidence.
   Enter investor conversations with a sharper story, stronger answers, and a clearer next step.

## Calls To Action

Primary CTA:

- Start your fundraising plan

Secondary CTAs:

- Analyze my pitch deck
- Find matching investors
- Generate investor outreach
- Practice VC questions

## Stakes

What founders avoid:

- Wasting weeks researching the wrong investors
- Sending generic cold emails that get ignored
- Walking into meetings without investor-specific context
- Discovering pitch weaknesses only after investor calls
- Losing confidence when hard questions come up

What founders gain:

- A focused investor target list
- A clearer fundraising narrative
- More relevant outreach
- Better meeting prep
- Confidence in the next investor conversation

## Section Copy

### Stop Fundraising In The Dark

Most founders spend too much time guessing which investors to contact, what to say, and whether their pitch is strong enough. RaiseSignal turns scattered fundraising work into a guided process.

### Find Investors Who Fit

Match your startup against investor stage, sector, check size, geography, thesis, and portfolio context so you can focus on the people most likely to care.

### Make Your Pitch Investor-Ready

Upload your deck and get feedback on the story investors evaluate: problem, market, traction, team, ask, and narrative strength.

### Send Outreach That Feels Personal

Generate cold emails, warm intro requests, and follow-ups that use investor-specific context instead of generic templates.

### Practice The Questions That Matter

Train against hard VC questions and get feedback on the substance, clarity, and delivery of your answers.

### Walk Into Meetings Prepared

Research target investors before the call with a concise "why them, why now" brief, portfolio context, conversation hooks, and likely objections.

## Voice And Tone

- Clear, direct, and founder-friendly
- Confident without hype
- Practical over inspirational
- Specific about outcomes
- Respectful of how high-stakes fundraising feels

## Copy Guardrails

- Do not promise guaranteed funding or investor introductions.
- Do not imply the tool replaces founder judgment.
- Avoid generic AI language like "supercharge" or "revolutionize."
- Keep copy focused on clarity, preparation, and fit.
- Position RaiseSignal as the guide, not the hero.
